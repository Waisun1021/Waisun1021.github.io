關於一起搭About TaxiSplit

一起搭由 Waisun 建立，起點是一個生活上的疑問：一起搭計程車時，直接把車資除以人數，會不會讓只搭一小段的人分攤太多？Waisun created TaxiSplit around an everyday question: when friends share a taxi, does an equal split make someone who rides only a short distance pay too much?

這個工具讓大家以相同、看得懂的公式比較分攤金額，再自行決定是否採用。分帳協議仍由共乘的人決定，網站不提供付款、叫車或帳務代管服務。The tool gives everyone a visible formula and comparable shares. Passengers choose their own agreement. The website does not provide payment processing, taxi booking or account management.
