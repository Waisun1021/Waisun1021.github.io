搭多少路，分多少車資。Share the fare by the distance you ride.

有人很快下車，有人搭到最後，總車資直接除以三不一定符合大家的期待。一起搭提供另一種分法：按各自實際搭乘距離的比例分攤。One friend gets out early while another rides to the final stop. An equal split may not suit everyone. TaxiSplit offers a sharing rule based on each person’s ride distance.

個人應付 ＝ 總車資 × 個人搭乘距離 ÷ 全員搭乘距離總和You pay = total fare × your ride distance ÷ everyone’s combined ride distance

例如總車資 300 元，三人搭 2、4、6 公里，分別付 50、100、150 元。這是一種可約定的分法，不代表所有共乘情境唯一公平的答案。For a TWD 300 fare and rides of 2, 4 and 6 km, pay TWD 50, 100 and 150. This is one agreed sharing rule, not the only fair rule for every trip.

三種行程，一個分帳方式Three trip modes

共同上車、不同下車：系統按離起點的開車距離，由近到遠建議下車順序。每人搭乘距離是起點到自己下車點的路段總和。Same pickup, different drop-offs: stops are suggested from nearest to farthest by driving distance from the origin. Each ride includes the segments up to that person’s drop-off.

不同上車、共同下車：系統由遠到近建議接人順序，每人搭乘距離從自己的上車点開始累計至共同目的地。Different pickups, same destination: pickups are suggested from farthest to nearest. Each ride includes the remaining segments from that person’s pickup to the shared destination.

不同上車、不同下車：每人填兩個地點，使用箭頭安排實際停靠順序；系統不允許先下車再上車。這種情境需要自己確認順序，不會自動安排最短路線。Different pickups and drop-offs: enter two places per person and use arrows to set the actual stop order. A person’s pickup must come before their drop-off. This mode does not optimize the route.

使用前先知道Before you calculate

車資由你輸入，不由網站估價。Google 地圖只協助估算開車距離；塞車、繞路、候車與過路費可能讓實際搭乘距離或收費不同。你也可以手動輸入相鄰路段的公里數。You enter the fare; this website does not estimate taxi prices. Google Maps estimates driving distance. Traffic, detours, waiting and tolls can affect the actual trip. Manual segment distances are also supported.

取整後各人金額仍等於總车資。搭乘距離最長者會被建議負責叫車，同距離會並列；網站不會實際叫車。Rounded shares add up to the entered total. The longest rider is suggested as the taxi booker, with ties listed together. The website does not book a taxi.
