計算資料與 Google 地圖Calculator data and Google Maps

姓名、總車資與分帳結果在你的瀏覽器中處理，本站不將它們寫入資料庫。使用自動距離時，你填入的上下車地點、行程模式與語言會傳送至本站的 Google Apps Script 服務，再由 Google Maps 查詢開車路線。Names, fares and allocations are processed in your browser and are not written to a site database. Automatic distance requests send your entered stops, trip mode and language to the site’s Google Apps Script service, which queries Google Maps driving routes.

手動模式不會為距離計算傳送你填入的地點給地圖服務。工具頁本身仍由 Google 提供，Google 可能處理網路請求與服務所需的技術資料。本站沒有要求 GPS、相機、麥克風、Gmail、聯絡人或 Google Drive 存取。Manual distance calculations do not send your entered stops to the map service. The calculator page is still hosted by Google, which may process technical information needed to deliver its services. The site does not request GPS, camera, microphone, Gmail, contacts or Drive access.
